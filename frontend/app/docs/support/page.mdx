## What we will never do

- Ask for your seed phrase, private key or passkey.
- Ask you to send funds to an address in a message.
- Guarantee a return.

Sacred has no token on offer. If someone says otherwise, or does any of the above in Sacred's name, it is not Sacred. Addresses on the [contracts page](/docs/contracts) are the only ones we publish.
