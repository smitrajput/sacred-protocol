# Funding Borrow

Borrow has its own investor pool, separate from Earn. Investors commit USDC for a year, the pool sells it to borrowers at fixed prices, and profit is shared by an agreed ratio. Nothing is guaranteed: not the return, the principal or an early exit. It is not open, and the mandate has not been approved.

## How it differs from Earn

| | Earn | Funding Borrow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Finances** | Trade positions, 14 days | Borrow sales, 30 days |
| **Commitment** | Daily withdrawal queue | 12 months |
| **Income** | Fixed share of markup | Share of actual distributable profit |
| **Protection** | Earned by the manager's cash waterfall | Investor-owned reserve, from investors' own profit |
| **Reference cash return** | 3.55% to 7.14% modeled APY | 2.39% to 4.05% APR while the reserve builds |

Each pool keeps its own ownership, receivables, reserves, loss carry, withdrawals and emergency permissions. Losses in one never reach the other.

## The mandate

- **Contract.** A restricted mudarabah. Investors provide capital, Sacred manages it, and profit is shared by a ratio agreed before the vintage starts.
- **Term.** 365 days. This is Sacred's liquidity and underwriting policy, not a universal Shariah requirement.
- **Vintages.** Ownership records are non-transferable, and each year's capital is its own vintage. New investors do not inherit old losses or buy old debts.
- **Fixed terms.** Your share is fixed when you subscribe and never changes within the vintage.

## Monthly profit

Every 30 days, realized profit after impairment and loss carry is distributed as an interim payment, subject to a cumulative true-up at the end. It is not a coupon. In the 75% reference, each 100 of distributable profit pays 60 to investors in cash, 15 into their reserve and 25 to the manager's escrow. Once the reserve is at target, the full 75 is paid in cash.

## The reserve is yours

The investment risk reserve belongs to the investors who funded it. It is not company equity and it is not free liquidity. [AAOIFI SS45](https://aaoifi.com/download/24233/) places such a reserve in the investors' profit entitlement. Sacred's own share of profit is not an automatic investor guarantee, and no manager guarantee is assumed. Manager misconduct, negligence or breach are a separate matter.

At exit, unused reserve returns to its owners. Keeping it for another year requires your explicit consent.

## When losses happen

Losses are recovered before fresh profit is shared. A reserve draw is a transfer, never income.

| Step | Principal | Reserve | Loss carry | Manager share |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Start | 100 | 10 | 0 | |
| A loss of 5 | 100 | 5 | 5 | |
| Next 5 of earnings restores the reserve | 100 | 10 | 0 | None |
| Following 10 of profit | 100 | 11.5 | 0 | 2.5, with 6 of investor cash |

Once a reserve is exhausted, any remaining impairment reduces principal. At runoff, unrecovered impairment belongs to that vintage. It does not pass to new investors. Sacred compensates misconduct or negligence under the contract, not routine investment losses.

## Runoff

In the model, the last new sale is on day 334. A 30-day sale matures on day 364, leaving a 24-hour cure to day 365. At the close:

1. New sales have stopped.
2. Debts are collected, and any impairment is allocated.
3. Interim distributions are trued up.
4. Collected principal, profit and unused reserve are returned to their owners.

Unresolved claims can delay final collection. Open claims keep non-transferable pro-rata recovery rights. There is no guaranteed early exit, and no debt-NAV token to sell. Rolling into a new vintage needs your consent.

## What you can expect

At the audited 8%/75% reference with $10m capital, cash APR is 4.05%, 3.34% or 2.39% at 85%, 70% or 50% use while the reserve builds. A first closed vintage returns 4.68%, 3.85% or 2.75% on original capital including returned reserve, with zero realized losses. [Pricing and reserves](/docs/borrow/economics) has the tables, which do not apply automatically to other prices or shares.

The pilot differs from these scale figures. Your funds are protected by 4 layers of backstops before a loss touches your principal: the borrower's collateral, the primary and tail reserve sleeves, and the [pilot backstop](/docs/risk#how-your-funds-are-protected). Total capacity is at most $250,000.

## Risks

- A reserve of 1% does not cover a day-long outage. Modeled annual ES99 for that emergency is 18.55% of financing.
- USDC can be frozen or depeg. The product's debts are in USDC units.
- A borrower default depends on selling collateral at a price, on Arc, through routes not yet measured.
- Twelve months is a long commitment for these returns. Whether investors want it is a demand test.
