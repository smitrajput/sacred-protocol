# Mechanism

One position is one credit sale of one asset to one trader, with the asset pledged back. The asset is a crypto asset held like a commodity, BTC or ETH at launch. Here is one from opening to its end, with every number. The running example throughout these docs: 1,000 USDC down on BTC at 3x for 14 days, at a yearly rate of 17%: the 15.5% markup plus the 1.5% gap premium on a 3x term position.

<Strip />

## Four parties, three contracts

| Party | Puts in | Gets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Depositors** | USDC, into a bucket they choose | 85% of the markup, paid as a 9% target with the rest held in a payout reserve; they bear the bucket's losses |
| **Manager** | Its work, plus a minimum deposit in each bucket | 10% of the markup, and half of any surplus above the target once the payout reserve is full |
| **Trader** | A down payment now and the balance by the due date | The asset, with 90% of any net profit and a loss capped at the down payment |
| **Spot market** | Nothing | Where the vault buys assets and where pledged assets are sold |

| Contract | Between | In one line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mudarabah** | Depositors and the manager | Depositors fund the bucket, the manager runs it, profit is split by a fixed ratio, loss falls on capital |
| **Murabaha** | The vault and the trader | The vault sells an asset it owns at cost plus a fixed markup, payable by a fixed date |
| **Rahn** | The trader and the vault | The trader pledges that asset back as security for the balance |

A Shariah board sits outside the flow: it approves the contract wording and the limits.

## Opening

The trader picks the asset, the down payment, the leverage, the term, and the highest all-in price per asset they will accept. Everything below happens in one transaction.

1. **Checks.** The bucket is open, leverage and term are within limits, and the bucket has enough idle USDC after queued withdrawals and the utilisation cap.
2. **The vault buys.** The bucket spends its own USDC, equal to the down payment times the leverage, on the spot market. The asset arrives at the vault, which now owns it. The amount spent is the cost.
3. **The vault sells.** The asset is sold to the trader at cost plus markup. The down payment moves to the bucket as the first part of the price. The rest is recorded as the balance.
4. **The pledge.** The asset moves into the position, pledged to the bucket.
5. **Price check.** If the sale price per asset is above the trader's limit, the whole transaction is undone and no funds move.

The order of steps 2 and 3 is a Shariah requirement: the vault must own the asset before it sells it. The position records cost and markup separately, because a murabaha seller must disclose the cost.

## What a position records

<Anatomy />

| Field | Example | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cost | 3,000.00 | What the vault paid, swap fees included |
| Down payment | 1,000.00 | Paid at opening |
| Markup | 13.04 | 2,000 × 17% × 14 ÷ 365. Fixed at opening |
| Balance (owed) | 2,013.04 | Cost plus markup, minus the down payment |
| Due | 14 days from opening | Fixed at opening |
| Status | Live | Live, closed, paid off, settled, or settled short |

Nothing changes after opening except the balance, which can only go down. The markup is the only charge. There is no opening, closing or late fee, and no funding. The rate itself is set by how full the bucket is when the position opens: 15.5% a year while the bucket is at or under 85% financed, rising in a straight line to 30% at the 92% cap, plus a gap premium of 1.5% on 3x term positions and 0.5% on margin positions for bearing the shortfall risk. Whatever the rate was at opening, it is fixed for the position's life.

## How a position ends

<Endings />

A trader who closes or pays off early is not charged for the unused days: waived amount = markup × days left ÷ term. A minimum of one day is always earned. The waiver is vault policy, not a term of the sale, which is one of the rulings on the [Shariah page](/docs/shariah).

Every sale of a pledged asset is on the open market, never to the vault; is protected by a floor of the price-feed value less 1%; repays whoever triggered it a fee indexed to gas at the time of the call, capped at 20 USDC, and carries no late penalty.

## Held to the due date

<Outcomes />

The bucket never earns more than the markup. Its profile is many small gains and a rare larger loss. The trader's loss stops at the down payment: in the last row the trader owes nothing more, and the bucket's 200 shortfall is absorbed in the order on the [risk page](/docs/risk). These results are before the trader's profit share: in the +20% row the profit is 586.96, so 58.70 goes to the backstop fund and the trader keeps 1,528.26, a gain of 52.8%. The share is 10% of *net* profit: a loss on an earlier position in the same bucket is carried forward for 90 days and set against later gains before anything is shared, and profit is measured at the price feed, not at the sale price.

Closed early, on day 5 with BTC up 10%: waived markup 13.04 × 9 ÷ 14 = 8.38, settlement amount 2,004.66, the trader takes 1,295.34, a gain of 29.5%. The bucket earns 4.66 for five days.

## Three modes, one position

The only difference between modes is whether the vault may sell the pledged asset before the due date.

| | Term | Margin | Evergreen |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Length** | 7 or 14 days | Up to 30 days | 365 days, pay only for days held |
| **Leverage** | BTC 3x, ETH 2x | 3x to 5x on BTC and ETH | BTC 3x, ETH 2x |
| **Early sale** | Never | Part sold if cover falls to 110%, enough to restore 115% | Part sold if cover falls to 110% |
| **Who carries a crash** | The trader's down payment, then the loss reserve, the backstop fund and staked SCR. Depositors only if all four run out ([order of loss](/docs/risk)) | Mostly the trader | Mostly the trader |

Cover is the asset's value divided by what the trader owes today. In margin mode only enough asset is sold to restore cover; the trader keeps the rest of the position, and can avoid a sale by part-paying or adding asset. A 10x perp on Hyperliquid is liquidated in full at about a 9% fall; a 5x margin position sells a third of the asset at a 12% fall and leaves the trader's equity intact.

The evergreen position removes the deadline: the markup for a full year is fixed at opening, the trader is charged only for the days used, and a far-away trigger (a 27% fall at BTC 3x on opening) protects the vault in place of a near due date.

## Properties ensuring sustainability

- The vault owns the asset before it sells it.
- A position's balance owed never increases. Its sale price, markup and due date never change.
- A pledged asset leaves a position only to the trader after full payment, or to the market. It is never sold to the vault, lent, staked or reused.
- A bucket never receives more than the settlement amount. Everything above goes to the trader, less the profit share.
- A trader never owes more than the pledged asset fetches.
- In term mode, only the trader's own action sells the asset before the due date.
- Buckets never share cash, shares, reserves or losses.
- Closing, paying off, settling and withdrawing can never be paused.
- No charge exists other than the markup and the repayment of actual costs.
- Every exit works without any server run by the manager.

Each is a line of code with a named test behind it; the list is on the [contracts page](/docs/contracts), and the [architecture page](/docs/architecture) shows the pieces that keep them.
