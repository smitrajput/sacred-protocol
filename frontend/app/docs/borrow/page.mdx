# How Borrow works

Borrow gives you spendable USDC against crypto you already hold. Trade pays for a purchase. Borrow gives you cash while your collateral stays yours. Under the hood it is a sale at a fixed price, known in dollars before you accept.

Borrow comes after Trade and stays disabled until its contracts are approved and its collateral routes are proven. Its capacity today is zero, and the launch price is not set. Figures below use an **8% annualized reference**, which is an audited model input. It is not a quote.

## A worked example

Amina pledges cirBTC worth $10,000 and takes the full 55%:

| Step | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| USDC she receives and can spend | 5,500 USDC |
| Fixed amount due on day 30 | 5,536.16 USDC |
| Amount to close on day 7 | 5,508.44 USDC |
| Future markup waived on day 7 | 27.73 USDC |
| Collateral value that triggers liquidation | $7,908.81 |

With WETH the limit is 50%: 5,000 USDC, 5,032.88 due on day 30, and liquidation at $7,742.89.

The collateral stays hers and is never reused. If some of it is sold, any surplus goes back to her. There is no late markup and no compounding.

## Limits

| Term | Proposed setting |
| --- | --- |
| **Supported collateral** | WETH and cirBTC on Arc are the candidates. Each is listed in [Arc's registry](https://docs.arc.io/arc/references/contract-addresses), and neither is enabled until its backing, feeds and depth are verified |
| **Borrowing capacity** | Up to 55% of your collateral's value on cirBTC, and up to 50% on WETH |
| **Term** | 30 days. 90 and 180 days are disabled |
| **Per borrower** | $1,000 to the lower of $5,000 or 2% of approved financing |
| **Pilot total** | Up to $250,000, at most $125,000 per collateral route |

These are upper limits. Each route opens at the lowest of its policy cap, its approved backing and feed capacity, and 25% of independently measured USDC bid depth within five minutes at 1% slippage. If no route qualifies, capacity is zero.

The per-borrower limit only works if Sacred can link one person's addresses. Limits by address alone are weaker, because one person can hold many addresses.

## Costs

The price is a fixed sale amount: `principal × (1 + quote × days ÷ 365)`. Pay early and a proposed binding rebate cancels the unused days:

```text
cash settlement = q × (1 + r × min(d, T) / 365)
binding rebate  = q × r × max(T − d, 0) / 365
```

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Rate** | Not set. The reference is 8% annualized, fixed once you accept |
| **Late charge** | None. No compounding and no automatic renewal |
| **Liquidation cost** | Actual execution costs and any separately approved, capped service fee. Sacred takes no penalty revenue |
| **Network** | Arc transaction costs, paid in USDC |

Sacred does not claim to be the cheapest. On $10,000 for 30 days, dated observations from late October 2026 cost about $36.99 on Aave V3 Core, $30.25 base on Aave's Arc V4 before any personal risk premium, and $41.00 on a large Morpho USDC/WBTC market. Sacred would cost $65.75 at 8%, $61.64 at 7.5% or $49.32 at 6%. Those rates are variable and can move. Sacred's price is fixed once you accept. New quotes are set against market costs, aiming for the lowest price Sacred can sustain. See [Pricing and reserves](/docs/borrow/economics).

## Repaying and getting your collateral back

Repay in part or in full at any time, in USDC. Each repayment pays off a matching slice of the sale. When the debt reaches zero your collateral is released. You can also withdraw collateral while borrowing, if fresh prices show principal LTV at or under the entry limit (55% on cirBTC, 50% on WETH) after the withdrawal, your full debt is healthy and nothing is overdue.

At maturity the full remaining debt is due. Healthy accounts get a 24-hour cure at no extra cost. After that the collateral is sold to settle the debt. A new sale is available only after real settlement, with its own underwriting and funding. An old debt is never rolled into a new one.

## Liquidation

```text
debt LTV = full remaining debt / collateral value
health   = liquidation LTV × collateral value / full remaining debt
```

| Collateral | Borrow up to | Liquidation starts at | Sale restores | Price fall before liquidation, from the limit |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| cirBTC | 55% | 70% | 60% | 20.9% |
| WETH | 50% | 65% | 55% | 22.6% |

Liquidation starts when debt LTV reaches the collateral's liquidation level: **70%** on cirBTC, a 20.9% fall from the example's opening value. Sacred sells only the collateral needed to bring debt LTV back to the restore level, **60%** on cirBTC, or to retire the debt if that is impossible or would leave under $250 of principal. Borrowing less than the limit leaves more room. The rebate applies to forced settlement exactly as it does to voluntary settlement, so you don't pay for the unused days in a liquidation either. Surplus collateral and proceeds return to you.

In the example, the trigger is reached when the $10,000 of collateral is worth $7,908.81 against $5,536.16 of debt. Sale proceeds pay the sale cost first, assumed at 0.10% plus $2, and the rest retires principal. The debt then falls by that principal's share of the fixed price.

| Liquidation on | Collateral sold | Sale cost | Applied to debt | Debt removed | Debt left | Collateral left |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Day 30, or without the rebate | $1,987.17 | $3.99 | $1,983.18 | $1,983.18 | $3,552.98 | $5,921.64 |
| Day 15, with the proposed rebate | $1,971.01 | $3.97 | $1,967.04 | $1,973.49 | $3,562.67 | $5,937.79 |

Both cases end at exactly 60% debt LTV. With the rebate, the debt falls by more than the cash applied, because the unused markup on the retired principal is waived. Without sale costs, the sale would be exactly a quarter of the collateral, $1,977.20.

You can add collateral or repay to avoid it. Those actions stay open even when new borrowing is frozen.

A liquidation needs valid independent prices and an executable quote within set limits. A price-triggered liquidation can still happen during the cure period.

In replay of the 30-day reference with every sale opened at its limit, 17.9% of BTC and 25.7% of ETH daily cohorts saw some price liquidation, and on average 6.9% and 10.0% of initial collateral was sold. At the earlier 45% limit those figures were 9.0% and 19.3%, so a higher limit means more frequent sales. The [risk page](/docs/risk#borrow) has the method.

## Keep in mind

- Collateral can be partly sold during a fall that later reverses.
- WETH and cirBTC on Arc also have bridge or custodian risk, which BTC and ETH prices don't show.
- Circle can block USDC at an address.
- Borrow is funded by Earn, through a vault contract kept apart from the one that funds Trade. A loss in one can't touch the other.

The contract sequence is in [Borrow mechanics](/docs/borrow/mechanism). What this sale still needs to be approved is on the [Shariah page](/docs/shariah#borrow).
