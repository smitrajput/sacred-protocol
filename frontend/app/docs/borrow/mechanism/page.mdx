# Borrow mechanics

Borrow is a fixed-price sale of USDC from inventory owned by the depositors in Earn's Borrow vault, payable later in USDC and secured by pledged collateral. It depends on a premise and on contracts that are not approved yet. This page lays out the steps those approvals would cover.

## The sequence

1. **Inventory.** Earn depositors who chose the Borrow vault, called investors here, hold USDC under a restricted mandate. This vault is a separate contract from the one that funds Trade. Sacred records which capital is committed, and acts as their manager and agent. The USDC is not company money.
2. **Pledge.** The borrower deposits approved collateral and grants a pledge and an enforcement mandate. The borrower keeps economic ownership of the collateral.
3. **Sale.** The borrower accepts a quote: a principal amount now, a fixed sale price, a maturity and a rebate rule.
4. **Delivery.** The pool transfers USDC to the borrower's wallet and records the debt in one step. The borrower can spend it freely. No simulated commodity trade is added in between.
5. **Rebate and settlement.** Payment within the term cancels unused markup under a binding *ibra*. Debt reaches zero and the collateral returns.

```text
fixed debt at sale         = F × (1 + r × T / 365)
cash settlement            = q × (1 + r × min(d, T) / 365)
binding rebate             = q × r × max(T − d, 0) / 365
remaining contractual debt = (remaining principal − q) × (1 + r × T / 365)
health factor              = liquidation LTV × collateral value in USDC / full remaining debt
```

`F` is principal, `r` the annualized quote, `T` the term in days and `q` principal retired on day `d`. Liquidation LTV is 70% for cirBTC and 65% for WETH.

The cash plus the waiver clears that slice's full fixed debt. This is Sacred's proposed linear formula, using a contractual approach supported by [Bank Negara Malaysia's Shariah Advisory Council](https://www.bnm.gov.my/-/resolutions-of-shariah-advisory-council-bank-negara-malaysia). BNM has not certified the formula.

## Why musawamah

Sacred uses **musawamah**, a negotiated-price sale, for pool inventory without documented acquisition cost. **Murabaha** needs real cost disclosure, and a $1 peg doesn't count as the cost of a lot. Murabaha applies only to lots with documented acquisition cost. This follows the [SBP model agreement](https://archive.sbp.org.pk/press/Essentials/Musawamah%20Facility%20Agreement.htm), which covers sales from an institution's existing inventory, separates acquisition from sale and allows collateral.

An independently funded new sale may follow real settlement. Prearranged circular refinancing is excluded, because [IIFA](https://iifa-aifi.org/en/49710.html) rejects replacing an old debt with a larger one for extra time. "Evergreen" here can only mean persistent collateral, reminders and fresh independent transactions.

If debt stays unpaid, collateral is enforced, or approved hardship forbearance applies at unchanged debt. There is no compounding, no late profit and no forced paper renewal.

## Liquidation

When debt LTV reaches the liquidation level (70% on cirBTC, 65% on WETH), a keeper sells a limited amount of collateral, applies proceeds to the rebated settlement amount, and restores 60% or 55% respectively, or closes the debt. The order of recovery is:

1. Approved execution costs
2. The secured sale obligation, with the approved rebate
3. Surplus back to the borrower

Unrecovered principal is recorded promptly. It is covered by the investor-owned reserves, primary first and then tail, and after that by the pool. Keeper rewards need an approved service arrangement. Model costs are 0.10% plus $2 in normal conditions, 3% plus $10 in four-hour stress and 5% plus $10 in day-long emergency, on $5,000 of financing. These costs are assumptions. They have not been measured on Arc, and none of them is penalty revenue.

## Collateral rules

- Never rehypothecated, staked or lent.
- Withdrawals need fresh prices, no unpaid maturity, principal LTV at or under the entry limit afterwards (55% on cirBTC, 50% on WETH) and a healthy full debt. Debt-free withdrawals need no price check.
- Adding collateral and repaying stay open during an origination freeze.
- Repayment in BTC or ETH needs an approved spot conversion at an actual settlement quote, and does not reset the debt.

## Later terms

| Setting | State |
| --- | --- |
| 90 and 180-day terms | Disabled. 8.5% and 9% are old illustrations and were never chosen as quotes |
| Entry limits above 55% on cirBTC or 50% on WETH | Excluded. In the four-hour stress replay a 60% limit either lost principal or left under 14% of price room |
| Automatic renewal | Excluded |

Longer terms have more liquidation risk and need matching investor maturities. In replay at the earlier 45% limit, 90 and 180-day cohorts saw price liquidation about 29% and 46% of the time on BTC, compared with 9% at 30 days. The longer terms were not rerun at the current limits.
