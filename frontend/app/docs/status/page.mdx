# Status and roadmap

Sacred is at the angel stage. This page says what exists, what does not, what each phase waits on, and which decisions are still open.

## Built

**Term mode, end to end.** The vault, the desk, the price reader and the backstop fund in Solidity; a keeper that settles due positions and runs the weekly cut-off; a read API that serves points; and the app at [/app](/app), where traders open, close, pay off and part-pay positions, depositors deposit and withdraw, stakers stake SCR, and SCR buyers buy from the reserve sale.

**The SCR side.** The token, its Uniswap v4 pool hook, the liquidity manager, the liquidity reserve, the reserve sale, the fee splitter and the staking fund, built and tested together against a real Uniswap v4 `PoolManager`.

**Margin mode** is in the same position code, switched off until the board rules on forced sale. No margin bucket exists.

## Deployed

Both sides are on Arbitrum One, every contract verified on Arbiscan; addresses on the [contracts page](/docs/contracts). A BTC bucket (3x max) and an ETH bucket (2x max) exist behind an allow-list. The token is on chain under a placeholder name, `SacredTokenDummy` with ticker `SRD`, with no airdrop, no listing and no protocol-owned stake. It is not live in any sense that matters.

## Tested

279 Foundry tests across unit, fuzz (1,000 runs), invariant (9 system invariants, 64 runs × 120 calls) and fork suites; 18 server tests including one end-to-end run; 20 frontend arithmetic tests; 4 browser flows driven through the real page with a real keeper and API. The fork test opens and closes a 3x BTC position on the real Uniswap v3 pool at the real Chainlink price. Two of eight arithmetic properties are proved by symbolic execution; the solver timed out on the other six, which are covered by fuzzing only.

## Not done

- **No audit.** None.
- **No Shariah board ruling.** Ten points plus SCR's go to a board before launch; the [Shariah page](/docs/shariah) lists them with odds and fallbacks.
- **No open access.** Depositors and traders are allow-listed. Deposits capped at 1 million USDC, raised in steps.
- **No token.** SCR launches only after the vault has run on points for 6 to 12 months and has real fees.
- **The launch economics are not yet in the deployed code.** The contracts on Arbitrum One run the first design's dials: a 10% markup plus 1% above 2x, a 30% profit share per position, all markup to depositors, a weekly withdrawal queue. The utilisation curve, the netted 10% profit share, the 85/10/5 split, the 9% target with its payout reserve, the admission and capital-return rules, the instant withdrawals and the fund buffer on the [economics page](/docs/economics) ship with the re-cut before launch, and the profit share is then tuned with launch data.

## Launch plan

1. **Term mode first.** BTC and ETH, 7 and 14 day positions, BTC 3x and ETH 2x.
2. **Allow-listed traders and depositors.** Deposits capped at 1 million USDC, raised in steps. The manager's own capital seeds each bucket. [Join the waitlist](/#waitlist).
3. **Points from day one.** No token.
4. **Margin, longer terms and evergreen positions** as rulings arrive and the reserve grows.
5. **SCR** after 6 to 12 months of real fees.

## Open decisions

**For the founder.** Confirm or change every "suggested" dial; the wrapped form of BTC; the cut-off day and hour; the utilisation curve; seed capital, bucket caps and position size limits; whether the position code is fixed for good or upgradable after a delay longer than the longest term.

**For the specification.** One contract per position or one ledger for all (the board may want the former; it would be a new desk). A fallback when a settlement sale cannot meet the floor: retry, then a short auction, never a sale to the vault.

**For the board.** The rulings on the Shariah page; whether profit may be measured against the previous high; who owns the loss reserve if a bucket closes.

**For counsel.** How the vault and the desk are classed under the UAE central bank law that names DeFi; whether "leverage" and "financing" can be used in marketing; whether a mutual shortfall fund needs an insurance licence; how Dubai's virtual-asset rules constrain the airdrop and reserve sales.

## Deferred past launch

- **Buckets on other chains**, starting with the USDC-only backstop fund and their own loss reserve, because a bucket elsewhere cannot call the staking fund in one transaction.
- **A staker discount on the profit share** and **staker votes on the risk dials**, both waiting on the board's ruling on the stakers' reward.
- **Yield on idle USDC** through a tokenised sukuk holding. No liquid on-chain Shariah money-market token was found; a 4% wrapper would lift the simulated payout from 9.0% to 9.6%.
- **Partial close by the trader.** The desk supports full close and part payment.
