# Borrow mechanics

Borrow is a fixed-price sale of USDC from inventory owned by the pool's investors, payable later in USDC and secured by pledged collateral. It rests on a premise and on contracts that are not yet approved. This page states the sequence those approvals would cover.

## The sequence

1. **Inventory.** Investors hold USDC under a restricted mandate. Sacred records which capital is committed, and acts as their manager and agent. The USDC is not company money.
2. **Pledge.** The borrower deposits approved collateral and grants a pledge and an enforcement mandate. The pool does not acquire economic ownership of it.
3. **Sale.** The borrower accepts a quote: a principal amount now, a fixed sale price, a maturity and a rebate rule.
4. **Delivery.** The pool transfers USDC to the borrower's wallet and records the debt in one step. The borrower can spend it freely. No simulated commodity movement is inserted.
5. **Rebate and settlement.** Payment within the term cancels unused markup under a binding *ibra*. Debt reaches zero and the collateral returns.

```text
fixed debt at sale         = F × (1 + r × T / 365)
cash settlement            = q × (1 + r × min(d, T) / 365)
binding rebate             = q × r × max(T − d, 0) / 365
remaining contractual debt = (remaining principal − q) × (1 + r × T / 365)
health factor              = 0.625 × collateral value in USDC / full remaining debt
```

`F` is principal, `r` the annualized quote, `T` the term in days and `q` principal retired on day `d`.

Cash plus waiver extinguishes that slice's full fixed debt. This is Sacred's proposed linear formula, using a contractual approach supported by [Bank Negara Malaysia's Shariah Advisory Council](https://www.bnm.gov.my/-/resolutions-of-shariah-advisory-council-bank-negara-malaysia). BNM has not certified the formula.

## Why musawamah

Sacred uses **musawamah**, a negotiated-price sale, for pool inventory without documented acquisition cost. **Murabaha** needs genuine cost disclosure, and a $1 peg is not a lot's cost. Murabaha applies only to lots with documented acquisition cost. This is the shape of the [SBP model agreement](https://archive.sbp.org.pk/press/Essentials/Musawamah%20Facility%20Agreement.htm), which covers sales from an institution's existing inventory, separates acquisition from sale and allows collateral.

## A sale's states

An independently funded new sale may follow real settlement. Prearranged circular refinancing is excluded, because [IIFA](https://iifa-aifi.org/en/49710.html) rejects replacing an old debt with a larger one for extra time. "Evergreen" here can only mean persistent collateral, reminders and fresh independent transactions.

If debt stays unpaid, collateral is enforced, or approved hardship forbearance applies at unchanged debt. There is no compounding, no late profit and no forced paper renewal.

## Liquidation

When debt LTV reaches 62.5%, a keeper sells a bounded amount of collateral, applies proceeds to the rebated settlement amount, and restores 50% or closes the debt. The order of recovery is:

1. Approved execution costs
2. The secured sale obligation, with the approved rebate
3. Surplus back to the borrower

Unrecovered principal is recorded promptly. It falls on investor-owned reserves, primary then tail, and then on the pool. Keeper rewards need an approved service arrangement. Model costs are 0.10% plus $2 in normal conditions, 3% plus $10 in four-hour stress and 5% plus $10 in day-long emergency, on $5,000 of financing. They are assumptions, not observed Arc fills or penalty revenue.

## Collateral rules

- Never rehypothecated, staked or lent.
- Withdrawals need fresh prices, no unpaid maturity, principal LTV of 45% or less afterwards and a healthy full debt. Debt-free withdrawals need no price check.
- Adding collateral and repaying stay open during an origination freeze.
- Repayment in BTC or ETH needs an approved spot conversion at an actual settlement quote, and does not reset the debt.

## Later terms

| Setting | State |
| --- | --- |
| 90 and 180-day terms | Disabled. 8.5% and 9% are old illustrations, not selected quotes |
| 50 / 65 / 55 LTV policy | Disabled candidate. Needs new approval |
| Automatic renewal | Excluded |

Longer terms carry more liquidation risk and need matching investor maturities. In replay, 90 and 180-day cohorts saw price liquidation about 29% and 46% of the time on BTC, against 9% at 30 days.
