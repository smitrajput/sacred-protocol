# Architecture

This page is the proposed system: two isolated vaults on Arc, with shared libraries. It is a specification. None of it is built on Arc. What exists today is the earlier term-mode system on Arbitrum One and Monad testnet, listed on the [contracts page](/docs/contracts).

| Layer | Proposed | Note |

## Isolation

**MarginVault** holds Earn deposits, BTC and ETH debt books, tier and vintage accounting, and earned protection. **CashFinanceVault** holds Borrow investors, receivables, investor-owned reserves, queues, loss carry and its emergency permissions.

Shared: reviewed libraries, token adapters, validated feeds, keeper and routing infrastructure, notifications, monitoring and UI. Neither vault gets spending approval over the other. No token or cross-product subsidy enters either forecast. Trade forecasts assume **zero** Borrow income.

## Trade engine

<Engine />

1. **Margin engine** executes genuine purchases and sales, stores debt and pledges, and calls ledger hooks on every economic path.
2. **Account ledger** keeps unitization, daily observations, exposure integrals, capital-days and a bounded 52-week ring.
3. **Score engine** computes the weekly [SIFT](/docs/trade/sift) skill label from canonical state. A separate daily transition reads the published ceiling table and repayment checkpoints.
4. **Capacity controller** enforces account and portfolio limits on every origination, counting pending commitments before external calls.
5. **Permissionless keepers** supply deterministic checkpoints and trigger reviews and partial sales. They cannot choose a score or rewrite accounting.
6. **Indexer and UI** display. They have no allocation authority.

Liquidation and settlement do not read the score. Coverage alone triggers a partial sale.

**Vault accounting.** Approved financing, including commitments, is checked against the 95% NAV cap. Originations stop at the 5% idle floor. New financing needs temporary purchase cash of `L/(L−1)` times the financing. Queued withdrawals rank ahead. Risk capacity is weighted:

```text
eligible_cash ≥ Σ (approved_financing_i × stock_ratio_i)
```

Primary and tail requirements are checked separately. Protection is manager-owned cash, separate from depositor idle cash and trader capital. Each subscription belongs to a vintage with fixed income entitlements, so a new allocation affects accepted new vintages only.

**Queues.** A bounded canonical request queue is registered per allocation epoch. Deterministic ordering runs through a gas-bounded batch or a proof binding the complete eligible queue, so an operator cannot omit competing requests.

## Borrow engine

Debt is integer **six-decimal USDC**. Arc's native USDC uses 18 decimals for the same balance, so values are normalized by 10¹² and never added together. WETH has 18 decimals and cirBTC 8, and each oracle's scale is normalized with checked arithmetic.

- **Agreed terms are versioned.** A new quote, cap or LTV never changes an existing sale.
- **Prices.** Feed-specific staleness is the documented heartbeat plus an approved grace. An hourly feed alone cannot meet the 60-second execution requirement.
- **Freeze.** New risk and unsafe collateral withdrawals stop. Repayment and top-ups continue.
- **Collateral is never reused**, and its surplus belongs to the borrower.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can | Cannot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Timelocked governance** | Change future policy within limits. Borrow risk increases wait 35 days | Change an existing sale or vintage entitlement |
| **Emergency guardian** | Tighten limits or pause new risk | Award more credit, delete a loss, or stop repayment and safe exits |
| **Keepers** | Checkpoints, reviews, partial sales, within bounded quotes | Set prices, choose scores, take surplus |
| **Manager** | Run the vaults, set new offers | Spend protection on operations, or use depositor principal as protection |

Rule and version hashes are persisted, and every capacity change emits a versioned event.

## Implementation notes

- Every arithmetic path is bounded and gas-tested. Available financing rounds down.
- The fixed-point score needs golden-vector parity with the reference model, including boundary tests.
- Stale or incomplete inputs fail closed. Safe exits survive origination pauses.
- Deposits cannot repair reputation, open losers stay visible, and wallet splitting cannot evade aggregate caps.
- Repayments and partial sales release usage exactly once. Reserve shortfalls block expansion.

Acceptance: numbers and calculators agree with the canonical parameters, cash ownership and collection timing are explicit, changing depositor terms affects accepted new vintages only, queues and caps are enforced, and contract review, security review and live execution come before activation.
