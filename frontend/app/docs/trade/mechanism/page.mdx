# Trade mechanics

A position is one genuine sale of one asset to one trader, with the asset pledged back as security. The running example is a $10,000 BTC purchase at 2× for 14 days. Every step below is proposed, and needs contract review and implementation.

<Strip />

## Four parties, three contracts

| Party | Puts in | Gets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Depositors** | USDC, into the MarginVault | 77% of base markup, as variable income. Four layers of backstops stand in front of their principal |
| **Manager** | Its work | 23% of base markup, paying operations first, then protection, then company cash |
| **Trader** | Their capital now, the balance by maturity | The asset, its full gains and losses, less the agreed price |
| **Spot market** | Nothing | Where assets are bought and pledged assets are sold |

| Contract | Between | In one line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mudarabah** | Depositors and the manager | Depositors fund the vault, the manager runs it, profit is split by a fixed ratio, loss falls on capital |
| **Murabaha** | The vault and the trader | The vault sells an asset it owns at disclosed cost plus a fixed profit, payable later |
| **Rahn** | The trader and the vault | The trader pledges the asset as security for the debt |

## Opening

Everything below happens in one transaction, in this order.

1. **Quote.** The trader sees the asset, price, markup in dollars, maturity, execution assumptions, coverage rules and the informational Hyperliquid comparison.
2. **Checks.** Fresh capital, the [SIFT](/docs/trade/sift) ceiling, portfolio and tier limits, withdrawal priority, oracle, depth and route. A cheaper competing quote is not a reason to refuse.
3. **Cash.** Acquisition preflight needs `L/(L−1)` times the new financing in temporary purchase cash, which is twice the financing at 2×, until the trader's capital arrives.
4. **The vault buys.** It pays for the asset on the spot market and owns it.
5. **The vault sells.** The asset is sold to the trader at cost plus a fixed markup. The trader's capital is the first part of the price, and the rest is a fixed debt.
6. **The pledge.** The asset is pledged as security and held in the position.

The vault must own the asset before it sells it. The contract records cost and markup separately, because Murabaha requires the cost to be disclosed.

<Anatomy />

```text
financing = asset_cost × (1 − 1/L)
markup    = financing × quoted_APR × days / 365
debt      = financing + markup
```

Full markup is booked once at origination. It is reversed only by an executed, approved rebate. Closing never charges it twice.

## Coverage and partial sale

```text
coverage = asset value / full remaining contractual debt
```

Full remaining debt includes scheduled markup under the separately approved rebate framework. At 2× a partial sale begins at 160% and restores 190%. Only the asset needed to restore coverage is sold, on the open market, and the trader keeps the rest. Remaining dust below $250 of financing closes.

<PartialSale />

The vault sells under a separately reviewed authority, on terms agreed at the sale. No debt is extended to collect markup. Execution, oracle and depth checks remain mandatory on every sale.

## Settlement

After approved rebates and actual close costs, every repayment and sale applies principal first:

```text
P = remaining principal
M = remaining markup
S = net recovered cash after close costs

principal recovered = min(P, max(S, 0))
principal shortfall = max(0, P − max(S, 0))
markup collected    = min(M, max(0, S − P))
trader surplus      = max(0, S − P − M)
```

Partial sales and repayments reconcile exactly once. Collection stops at the pledged asset, so a trader never owes more than the pledge. A principal shortfall is recorded even when a reserve absorbs it, because a reserve draw does not erase an economic loss. Impairments are processed before distributions, share minting and redemptions.

Renewal is settlement followed by a genuine new purchase and sale at a new quote. There is no rollover.

## Properties to keep

- The vault owns the asset before it sells it.
- A sale's price, markup and maturity never change after the sale.
- A pledged asset leaves only to the trader after payment, or to the market. It is never sold to the vault, lent, staked or reused.
- The trader keeps all surplus above the debt. No mandatory share of profit exists at any tier.
- Depositor principal and refundable amounts never become protocol protection.
- Repayment and exit survive origination pauses.

These are design requirements for the proposed engine. The [contracts page](/docs/contracts) separates them from what is built today.

## The leverage ladder

All terms are 14 days, mandatory profit share is 0% at every tier, and leverage goes up to 5×. Higher tiers are gradually opened as their protection is earned and Shariah rulings approved.

| Leverage | Financed APR | Normal depositor share | Trigger / restore | Initial price room | Primary + tail stock | Cap on approved financing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **2×** | **10.0%** | **77.00%** | **160% / 190%** | **19.7%** | **0.4% + 0.6% = 1.0%** | |
| 2.5× | 11.0% | 74.55% | 145% / 160% | 12.6% | 0.5% + 1.0% = 1.5% | all above 2×: 25% |
| 3× | 12.5% | 71.60% | 130% / 145% | 12.9% | 1.5% + 7.5% = 9.0% | 3× and up: 15% |
| 3.5× | 14.0% | 69.29% | 125% / 135% | 10.2% | 2.5% + 7.5% = 10.0% | 3.5× and up: 7.5% |
| 4× | 16.0% | 66.88% | 120% / 130% | 9.4% | 4.5% + 9.5% = 14.0% | 4× and up: 5% |
| 4.5× | 18.0% | 65.00% | 116% / 125% | 9.1% | 6.5% + 10.5% = 17.0% | 4.5× and up: 2.5% |
| 5× | 20.0% | 63.50% | 115% / 123% | 7.2% | 6.5% + 10.5% = 17.0% | 5×: 1% |

`normal depositor share = (0.077 + 0.5 × (APR − 0.10)) / APR`. Stock ratios apply to approved financing, not asset notional or revenue. Caps overlap and count current exposure and committed financing. Rates and triggers are policy choices informed by replay, not a fitted commercial optimum. The 3× trigger at 130% gives traders more price room than 135%, and its 9% stock pays for the resulting stress exposure.

## Not in this design

| Left out | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Shorts | Selling what one does not hold needs a prepaid forward (*salam*) with its own market and rulings |
| Rollover | Extending a debt for an extra charge is riba |
| Synthetic perps | Nothing is owned, and settlement is in cash |
| Mandatory trader profit share | It is not established as part of a known sale price |
| Automatic higher leverage from a good score | Score and tier are separate decisions |
